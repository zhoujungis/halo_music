Changelog

Major changes to Halo Music. The complete history is on GitHub Releases.

v2.7 · English interface

  • Chinese/English UI with a manual switch, saved per browser
  • Automatic language selection based on the device time zone, with a country fallback
  • English charts (US / UK / AU) refreshed daily, played through the same source
  • English recommendations: international songs and artists
  • Playlist share links and a listening-report share card

v2.3 · Discovery and stats

  • Charts moved to their own page: Top Hits / New Releases / Trending, refreshed every night
  • New "Recommended songs", "Recommended artists", and local search history on Discover
  • "My Info" rebuilt as listening stats: total time, 7-day trend, listening hours, top artists and songs
  • Quality labels only show confirmed information — no "lossless" claim when unverified
  • Buffering and error states, progress dragging, volume shortcuts, and cover transitions in the player

v2.2 · Playback and apps

  • Preview clips automatically switch to the full track, and the swap is remembered
  • Playback failures skip to the next song; repeated failures stop the skip
  • Play icons across every list follow the real playback state
  • Android app supports background playback, lock screen, and notification controls
  • Playlist descriptions and automatic covers; the download page shows version, size, and release notes

Earlier

  • Accounts: email-code sign-up, Google sign-in, cloud sync for favorites and playlists
  • Playlist import from NetEase Cloud Music, QQ Music, and Qishui Music
  • Multi-platform aggregated search with de-duplication, synced lyrics
  • Windows and Android apps
