About Halo Music

Halo Music is a lightweight, cross-platform personal music space. It is not a social network and doesn't chase catalogue size — it focuses on three things: finding the song you want, playing it smoothly, and keeping your favorites and listening history in order.

What it is

  • Aggregated search and playback: one search shows public results from multiple platforms, lyrics scroll in sync, and playback falls back to another source when one fails.
  • Favorites and playlists: save songs, build playlists, import public playlists from NetEase Cloud Music, QQ Music, and Qishui Music, and export a backup.
  • Listening stats: total plays, total time, a 7-day trend, your usual listening hours, and your most-played artists and songs.
  • Everywhere: use the web app instantly; Windows and Android builds are available; on iPhone you can add it to your Home Screen for a full-screen experience.

What it is not

Halo Music is not a music download site. It stores and creates no audio files; it only displays public search results from third-party platforms and proxies their playback URLs. Music, covers, and lyrics belong to their respective platforms and creators. See the disclaimer in the app for details.

Accounts and data

Search and playback need no account; an account is only required to sync favorites, playlists, and listening history. Passwords are stored as one-way hashes and sessions use HttpOnly cookies. See the privacy policy for the full data practices.
