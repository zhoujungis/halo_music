Contact

Halo Music is an independently maintained personal project. Feature requests, bug reports, and copyright requests are all welcome through the channels below.

Bugs and suggestions

File a GitHub Issue with the steps to reproduce, the page where it happened, and your browser version — that makes it much easier to track down.

Open an issue →

Or email edfc1582@shmarpe.resend.app (usually answered within a day or two).

Copyright requests

If you believe a search result or proxied playback URL infringes your rights, open an issue with the page, the song details, and who you are. Verified results are removed promptly.

The site stores and creates no audio files; it only displays public search results from third-party platforms. See the disclaimer.

Data export and account issues

To export your favorites, playlists, and listening history, or if something is wrong with your account, open an issue describing the situation and include your username (never your password).
