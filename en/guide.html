Guide

This guide covers the main things you can do in Halo Music. Everything works in the browser — nothing to install.

Search and playback

  1. Type a song, artist, or keyword in the box at the top and press Enter (or tap Search).
  2. Results come from several platforms at once and are de-duplicated; tap any result to play.
  3. If a source fails, the player tries other available sources; if none work it skips to the next track.

The player shows its state as loading / playing / paused / buffering / failed so you always know what's happening.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Space: play / pause
  • P / N: previous / next track
  • F: favorite the current song
  • ← / →: seek back / forward 5 seconds (hold to keep seeking)
  • ↑ / ↓: volume up / down 5%
  • M: mute / unmute

Queue and playlists

Use the button on each search result to add it to the queue. Once signed in you can also:

  • Create, edit, and delete your own playlists, with an optional description
  • Import songs from public playlist links on NetEase Cloud Music, QQ Music, and Qishui Music
  • Export favorites and playlists to a JSON file as a backup

Listening stats

When signed in, "My Info" shows your listening stats: total plays, total time, plays this month, a 7-day trend, your usual listening hours, and your most-played artists and songs. All of it comes from your own play history and is never used for ad profiling.

FAQ

  • Why do some songs only play for a few seconds? Free sources only provide a preview for some licensed tracks. The player detects this and tries to switch to a full version; if it can't, it skips to the next song.
  • Why is loading sometimes slow? Audio comes from third-party platforms, so resolving and proxying takes time; the player retries other routes automatically.
  • Will my favorites still be there on another device? Sign in with the same account to sync favorites and playlists.
  • Can I download audio? No. The download button only saves search results to your local favorites backup.
