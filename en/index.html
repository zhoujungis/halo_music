Halo Music

A lightweight, cross-platform personal music space. Search across public sources, play instantly, and keep your favorites, playlists, and listening history in one place.

What you can do

  • Search and play: one search returns public results from multiple platforms, with synced lyrics and automatic fallback when a source fails.
  • Favorites and playlists: save songs, build playlists, import public playlists, and export a backup.
  • Listening stats: total plays and time, a 7-day trend, your usual listening hours, and your most-played artists and songs.
  • Everywhere: use the web app instantly, install the Windows or Android build, or add the site to your iPhone Home Screen.

Start listening

The player needs no account. Favorites, playlists, and listening stats sync across devices once you sign in.
